----------------------------

Q: How long does implementation take?
A: VoiceAgent Pro can be set up in 2-3 days. InsightIQ and ChatAssist take 1 week. DocuSense custom implementations take 2-3 weeks depending on document complexity.

Q: Do you offer a free trial?
A: Yes! All our products come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial period.

Q: Can the VoiceAgent speak in Hindi or regional languages?
A: Yes. VoiceAgent Pro supports English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi. Additional languages can be added on request with a 2-week turnaround.

Q: Is our data secure?
A: Absolutely. We are ISO 27001 certified and compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256. We offer on-premise deployment for enterprise clients who require complete data sovereignty.

Q: Can I integrate your products with my existing CRM?
A: Yes. VoiceAgent Pro natively integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Freshsales and Pipedrive. Custom integrations via REST APIs are available for all plans.

Q: What kind of support do you provide?
A: Starter plans receive email support with a 24-hour response SLA. Growth plans get priority support with a 4-hour SLA. Enterprise clients get a dedicated customer success manager and 24/7 phone and email support.

Q: Do you offer custom AI model training?
A: Yes. For enterprise clients, we offer custom fine-tuning of AI models on your proprietary data to improve accuracy and brand alignment. This is available as an add-on service.

Q: What is your uptime guarantee?
A: We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all SaaS products. Enterprise clients can negotiate a 99.95% SLA with penalty clauses.
